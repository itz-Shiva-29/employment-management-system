About these terms

These terms apply to anyone who uses the Employment Management System, including administrators, HR staff and employees, and to anyone who visits this project page. The organisation that operates your installation may have its own workplace policies, which also apply to you.

Accounts and access

  • Accounts are created by the organisation’s administrator or HR staff. Each account has one role: administrator, HR or employee.
  • Keep your password private and do not let anyone else use your account. Tell your administrator straight away if you think someone else has used it.
  • Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared computer. Sessions also end after 60 minutes of inactivity.
  • The organisation can deactivate an account at any time, for example when someone leaves. A deactivated account can no longer sign in.

Acceptable use

  • Use the application only for your own work and only within the access your role gives you.
  • Do not try to reach pages, records or payslips that belong to someone else, or to get around the role checks.
  • Do not enter false information, such as incorrect attendance or leave details.
  • Do not post anything unlawful, abusive or confidential to people who should not see it in notices, leave reasons or remarks.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt, overload or damage the application or the server it runs on.

Accuracy of records

Records are only as accurate as the information entered. Employees should keep their contact details up to date in My profile and report other errors to their administrator or HR team. Administrators and HR staff are responsible for the records they create and change, and these changes are recorded in the activity log.

Payroll calculations

The application calculates pay from the salary structure an administrator enters. It applies employee provident fund at the configured 10% or 12% rate up to the wage ceiling, and Gujarat professional tax. It also prorates the joining month by scheduled working days. Statutory rules, eligibility and exceptions change, and the application does not check them for you. Administrators must confirm payroll figures before paying salaries or filing statutory returns.

Availability and data

The organisation that operates the installation is responsible for running the server, keeping it secure and making backups. The application may be unavailable during maintenance or because of problems with that server. How information is handled is explained in the privacy policy.
